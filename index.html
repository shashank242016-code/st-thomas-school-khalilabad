A story that began in 1988

Built around learning, character and community.

St. Thomas School began in 1988 under the Catholic Diocese of Gorakhpur with 20 students and two teaching staff in rented premises. Over the years, the school grew into an institution serving learners from LKG through XII, with ICSE/ISC-oriented academics and a broad campus life.

About the school

A history of steady growth.

From a small beginning in 1988 to a full LKG–XII institution serving a large student community.

Milestones

From 20 students to a thriving school community.

The timeline below brings together key milestones described on the school's public website.

1988

The beginning

The school was started by the Catholic Diocese of Gorakhpur with 20 students and two teaching staff in rented premises.

2003

Permanent affiliation

The school received permanent affiliation.

2004

Intermediate classes

Inter classes commenced as the school expanded its academic offering.

Later years

Growing into an institution

The school continued expanding its facilities, programmes and student community, eventually serving LKG through XII.
