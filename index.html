A story that began in 1988

Built around learning, character and community.

St. Thomas School began in 1988 under the Catholic Diocese of Gorakhpur with just 20 students and two teaching staff in rented premises. Today it serves learners from LKG through XII.

1988

The school received permanent affiliation to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations.

2004

Inter classes commenced as the school expanded its academic offering to senior secondary.

Curriculum

Curiosity, discipline & opportunity.

The course of study follows the ICSE/ISC framework prescribed by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations.

Kindergarten

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Drawing, Physical Education — with focus on reading, rhymes, conversation and dictation.

Primary (LKG – V)

English & Hindi (Language & Literature), Mathematics, General Science, Environmental Studies, Social Studies, Computer Studies, Art, Music & Dance, Craft, Value Education, Physical Education.

High School (VI – X)

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History & Civics, Geography, Computer Applications, Sanskrit, Environmental Education, SUPW, Moral Science, Physical Education, General Knowledge.

Commerce stream:
• English, Mathematics/Hindi, Accountancy, Economics, Commerce

Admission Process

Admission depends on availability of seats and merit. Registration does not guarantee admission.

LKG Requirements

  • Child must have completed 4 years on 31st March of the year of admission
  • Date of Birth Certificate (Baptism certificate for Christian children / Govt. issued birth certificate)
  • Photograph of parents with child + passport size photo of child
  • Child should recognize English & Hindi alphabets, count 1–20, and identify colours, flowers, animals, birds, fruits
  • Both parents must accompany the child for identification

UKG – Class VIII

  • Date of Birth Certificate, Transfer Certificate, Character Certificate, Annual Report Card
  • For classes above VI: only from recognized English medium schools
  • Written Test + Interview (minimum 60% in each subject required)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from Government Physician

Class XI (ISC)

  • Minimum 70% in Class X Board + Preliminary exams
  • At least 70% in each subject opted for ISC
  • Good conduct, B grade in SUPW, 80% attendance in IX & X
  • Selection is not automatic even for internal students
Fee Structure

Transparent & Structured

All payments must be made before the dates specified in the fee book. Keep receipts safely.

Important Notes

  • Application processing fee: ₹400 (new students only)
  • Board charges extra for Classes IX–XII
  • Exam fee is remitted in 2nd, 3rd & 4th installments
  • Composite fee is paid quarterly in four installments
  • Late fee fine: ₹100 per month
  • No student is permitted to begin a new term unless all arrears are cleared

Dress Code

School Uniform

LKG & UKG

Grey colour trousers and grey-white check shirt, tie, belt and shoes (for both boys and girls).

Class I – V (Summer)

Boys: Dark Navy Blue long pants, half-sleeved white shirt, tie, belt, black leather shoes with blue socks.
Girls: Dark Navy blue pinafore, half-sleeved white shirt + same accessories.

Class VI – X (Summer)

Boys: Dark Navy Blue long pants, half-sleeved white shirt, tie, belt, black shoes.
Girls: Dark Navy blue skirt, half-sleeved white shirt + same accessories.

Winter

Dark Navy Blue half sweater and woollen blazer with monogram (for all). Only readymade items allowed — home-knit sweaters not permitted.

PT Dress (Wednesday)

White shirt, white pants/skirt, school tie, belt, white canvas shoes and white socks.

House Uniform (Saturday)

Colour half-sleeved shirt according to house with school emblem, white pants/skirt, white canvas shoes.
