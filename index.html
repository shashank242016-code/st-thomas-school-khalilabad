A story that began in 1988

Built around learning, character and community.

St. Thomas School began in 1988 under the Catholic Diocese of Gorakhpur with just 20 students and two teaching staff. Today it stands as a full LKG–XII institution with ICSE/ISC academics and a vibrant campus life.
